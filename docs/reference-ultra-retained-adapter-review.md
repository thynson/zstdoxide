retained bounds. Removing them reconstructs the exact predecessor helper. The
source domain becomes 128..32768 bytes with two to four 64..8192-byte blocks;
packet capacity becomes source-cap plus 72 and frame capacity source-cap plus 64.

bounded replay and minimum-three-byte match contract. A source-aware conservative

alternative after `withMoreLiterals` is computed. Removing all substitutions
