## Interrupted execution and recovery

An earlier compilation exhausted disk before running tests. Its failed log and
cleanup history remain archived. The successful matrix was later interrupted
by the application after ten full products and ten `no_std` products. The shell
handle disappeared and process inspection found no remaining driver/compiler.
The original interrupted log and cache were preserved.

The first recovery attempt rejected a changed launcher PATH before building.
Its successor used the original recorded effective environment and a fresh
cache. The eleventh full product passed, but a driver comparison confused JSON
lists with newly parsed tuples. Independent full-group and normalized-field
comparison established identical results. An additive successor retained those
21 successful commands, completed the pending `no_std` command, and ran every
remaining product. Both checkpoint records, all recovery drivers and the
independent recovery checker are retained in the archive. No failed or partial
command is relabeled as a passed execution.

The completed matrix reconciles native and foreign-platform evidence through
the existing explicit Rustdoc-only final-source binding. Historical runs retain
their actual source identities. Actual timing and independent repeat measurement
remain a separate work item.
